# loldb - League of Legends Statistics & Insights

loldb is a public statistical analysis and insights database designed for the League of Legends community. Our primary goal is to enhance player experience by providing comprehensive statistics, deep gameplay insights, and performance correlations extracted from a large dataset of matches.

## About

By leveraging Riot Games APIs (including Match-V5, Summoner, and Champion APIs), loldb aggregates detailed match timelines, player behaviors, and objective events to offer novel insights, such as:

- Impact of vision control around major objectives (Baron Nashor, Dragon)
- Influence of specific in-game actions on winning outcomes
- Data-driven strategies and gameplay trends

Players and analysts can explore these insights to improve their gameplay based on reliable and statistically significant information.

## Project Status

Currently, loldb is in the data collection phase to build a comprehensive, publicly accessible database. All data presented strictly adheres to Riot's policies, maintaining player anonymity and focusing exclusively on constructive insights and improvement.
